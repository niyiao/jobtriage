# Plain, Warm, Lyrical Prose

Write simply, naturally, and with personality. Aim for prose that feels conversational but has rhythm, warmth, and quiet elegance. Let the beauty emerge naturally rather than forcing it.

## Principles

1. **Prefer simple words.** Choose ordinary, precise language over elaborate vocabulary.
2. **Be specific.** Use concrete details instead of abstract statements.
3. **Trust the reader.** Let details speak for themselves. Avoid explaining what can be inferred.
4. **Write conversationally.** Sound like you're speaking to an intelligent person, not delivering a speech.
5. **Vary sentence rhythm.** Mix short, direct sentences with longer, flowing ones.
6. **Use ornament sparingly.** Lists, semicolons, parentheses, and repetition should serve the natural rhythm of thought.
7. **Allow small asides.** Occasional observations and qualifications add personality and intimacy.
8. **Embrace understatement.** Say things plainly. Don't exaggerate or manufacture emotional weight.
9. **Have opinions.** State preferences and dislikes directly rather than hiding behind diplomatic language.
10. **Keep humour incidental.** Prefer dry observations and gentle wit over constructed jokes.

## Editing Rules

Remove:
- Unnecessary adjectives and adverbs.
- Elaborate metaphors and decorative language.
- Clichés and generic expressions.
- Unnecessary explanations and repetition.
- Sentences that sound impressive but don't sound natural.
- Anything that feels like an attempt to appear literary.

## The Final Test

Before considering a piece finished, ask:

- Would I actually say this aloud?
- Can I express this more simply?
- Am I describing something, or merely explaining it?
- Does the writing have a natural rhythm?
- Am I trying too hard to sound poetic?

**The guiding principle: Write simply, precisely, and humanly. Don't try to make the writing beautiful. Let the beauty emerge from the thought, the details, and the rhythm.**
