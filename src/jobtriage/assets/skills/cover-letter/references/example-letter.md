# Example letter (invented person)

This is a reference, not a letter in the user's `letters/` folder. The
person, company and facts are invented — never copy them into a real
letter. Read it for shape and tone: two body paragraphs, one concrete
piece of evidence, the posting's own language, no humour.

The posting: Northwind Studio, Senior Frontend Engineer, remote in the
EU, a design-tool company. The profile says the person has six years of
React and TypeScript, built a design system, and cares about
accessibility. The letter below uses only those facts.

---

Dear hiring team,

I build the tools designers and engineers share, and Northwind's
canvas is the kind of surface I have spent the last few years on. At
Meridian I led the frontend of a design-system migration across four
product teams: React and TypeScript, a shared token layer, and a
codemod that moved 1,200 components without a freeze on feature work.
The part I cared about most was the least visible — keyboard
navigation and focus behaviour stayed correct through the migration,
and the accessibility audit at the end found no new blockers.

Northwind's posting asks for someone who can hold a component API
steady while the product moves. That is the work I want: close to the
canvas, close to the people using it, and measured by whether the
tool disappears in their hands. I am based in Lisbon, available after
four weeks' notice, and happy to walk through the migration and its
trade-offs in an interview.

Sincerely,
Jordan Mensah
